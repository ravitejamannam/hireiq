# Reviewer instructions

RESEARCH_ONLY. These steps collect a human relevance judgment. They are not a hiring decision and not a training dataset.

Two different people must do this. Do not share your choice before the other person has finished. Do not use the same signed-in browser for both people.

## Who does what

Person A signs in as `member.nimbus@hireiq.local` (Client user 3).

Person B signs in as `owner.nimbus@hireiq.local` (Client user 2).

Use the local demo password you already have. Sign out completely before the other person starts.

## What to open

Use only these two addresses, on the local site:

1. http://localhost:4000/client/requirements/1/relevance/5
2. http://localhost:4000/client/requirements/1/relevance/7

Do not open the ordinary candidate page, the pipeline, assessments, evaluations, or any retrieval or ranking screen.

## What to do on each page

1. Read the job requirements and the candidate evidence on that page.
2. Answer the question on the page.
3. Click either **Relevant** or **Not relevant**. Click only one button, once.
4. Wait until the page says the judgment was recorded.
5. Do the same on the other address.
6. Sign out.

Choose from what you see on that page. There is no expected answer. Do not discuss the two applications with the other reviewer until both of you have signed out.

Clicking the other button on the same open page does not record a second choice. It repeats the first one. If you click the wrong button, stop and tell the researcher. Do not keep clicking.
